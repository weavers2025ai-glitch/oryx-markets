Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I earn as an affiliate?

There is no limit. You earn 10% with 1–5 active clients, 15% with 6–25, 20% with 26–50 and 30% with 51+, recurring for as long as your clients stay active.

When and how do I get paid?

Commissions are calculated monthly and paid within the first 10 business days of the following month. Minimum payout is $100, by bank transfer or local wallet.

Is there a cost to join?

No. The program is completely free — no setup fees and no monthly charges.

How long is the cookie duration?

90 days. If someone clicks your link and registers within 90 days, they are tracked to your account.

Do I earn on ongoing activity?

Yes. Your commission is recurring — you earn on your clients' continuing activity, not only their first month.

Who can join the affiliate program?

Anyone — bloggers, influencers, educators, consultants, community leaders and existing Oryx clients.
