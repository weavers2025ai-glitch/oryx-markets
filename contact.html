Common Questions

How do I open an account?

Register with your mobile and email, verify both OTP codes, and your account is approved by our team — usually within 24 hours. Your $10 welcome credit is added on approval.

How do I add balance?

Open your wallet, choose Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer, send the payment to the details shown, and submit your transaction ID. Our team verifies and credits your account.

How long do withdrawals take?

Approved withdrawals are processed back to your own payment method, with a full record in your wallet history.

Where can I learn the platform?

The User Guide covers the terminal, wallet, KYC, offers and more, step by step.
