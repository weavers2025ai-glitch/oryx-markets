FAQ

Questions, Answered

Who can become an Oryx IB?

Anyone with a trading audience — educators, signal providers, community leaders, content creators and existing brokers' partners. You apply below, we review, and your tracking link is issued on approval.

How is my rebate calculated?

You earn a fixed rebate for every lot your referred clients trade, on every account type. On ECN accounts you also receive a percentage share of the trading commission. Both are tracked in real time in your IB dashboard.

When and how do I get paid?

Payouts are processed within 24 hours of your request, to the same methods we offer clients — bank transfer and local wallets. There is no minimum payout for approved IBs.

Can I have sub-IBs?

Yes. Pro and Master IBs can invite sub-IBs and earn an override on their volume, all visible in one dashboard.

Do my clients pay more?

No. Your clients trade on the same spreads and conditions as everyone else. Your rebate comes from our margin, never from your clients' costs.
