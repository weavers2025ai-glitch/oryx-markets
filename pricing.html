Yes. After your account is approved you receive a $10 welcome credit to start trading. Company credits are for trading only and cannot be withdrawn.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Your plan follows your deposit level — deposit more and your account moves up automatically.

No hidden fees from us. Your deposits, trades and withdrawals are all recorded clearly in your wallet.
